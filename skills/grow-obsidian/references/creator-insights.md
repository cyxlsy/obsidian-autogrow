# Creator insights

Keep `features.creatorInsights.enabled=false` by default.

When enabled, analyze only newly created or updated distilled notes, their declared outcomes and unresolved questions, and explicit `focusAreas`.

Do not analyze keystrokes, application history, browsing history, private raw conversations, or unapproved directories.

Generate suggestions rather than behavioral judgments:

- recurring problems worth explaining publicly;
- reusable techniques;
- unresolved tensions that could become experiments;
- related projects that could be combined;
- candidate articles, tools, videos, or developer utilities.

Label every suggestion as an inference and cite the distilled notes supporting it. Never diagnose motivation, personality, productivity, or mental state.
