# Discovery and taxonomy

Discovery is opt-in and metadata-only.

Before running `Discover`, show the exact roots, explain that names, extensions, sizes, counts, and modification dates will be inspected, and require `discovery.consentGranted=true`. Never replace a root with its parent drive.

Use the inventory to suggest active project roots, archives, reference folders, generated-artifact folders, and candidate Obsidian categories. Present recommendations before modifying the vault. Prefer five to ten durable categories; do not create a category for every folder.

Keep storage and knowledge classification separate: a source folder may contain several topics, and several folders may contribute to one canonical note.
